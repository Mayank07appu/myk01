Velox Dynamic Pricing Architecture Specs

1. Algorithmic Purpose & Objectives

The engine balances rider demand with driver dispatch supply across dynamic urban zones. Rather than relying exclusively on opaque surge multiplier auctions, Velox decouples fare calculation into transparent, deterministic components:

  • Base Flagdrop & Mileage: Covers fixed operational vehicle depreciation.
  • Congestion Time Lag: Compares real-time route duration against baseline free-flow velocity.
  • Driver Weather Hazard Stipend: 100% of weather hazard fees are routed directly to drivers navigating inclement conditions.

2. Ethical Anti-Gouging Caps & Guardrails

Ride-hailing algorithms have historically faced public scrutiny for exorbitant pricing during emergencies or disasters. Velox enforces two algorithmic hard stops:

  • Maximum Surge Ceiling: Net combined surge multiplier cannot exceed 3.50x under standard commercial operation.
  • Statutory Emergency Freeze: Immediate override switch that freezes surge at 1.00x upon municipal declaration of emergencies or mass transit disruptions.

3. Technology & Deployment Stack

Built with zero external compilation dependencies. Pure HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, and Chart.js. Fully responsive and ready for instantaneous deployment on GitHub Pages.
